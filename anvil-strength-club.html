Programs

Pick the format that fits your goal. Every program is coached and logged, and you can move between them as you progress.

Strength Floor

Squat, bench, deadlift and overhead press with a coach-set progression. Your loads are logged every session so you can see the numbers go up.

5 × 5 60 min

Fat-Loss Circuit

Timed conditioning blocks with sleds, kettlebells and rowers. Work hard, rest briefly, repeat. Scaled for every fitness level.

40s / 20s 45 min

Powerlifting Club

Competition-style training on the platform with chalk allowed. Meet preparation, attempt selection and technique review on video.

1RM 90 min

Women's Batch

A women-only morning batch led by a woman coach. Strength basics, posture work and a relaxed pace for first-timers.

3 × 10 60 min

Mobility & Core

Hips, shoulders and trunk work that keeps you lifting without pain. Included with every membership, and a good first class after a long day.

Before you join

I have never lifted. Is that fine?

Yes. Every new member gets a first-week plan with a coach, and the Strength Floor starts with empty-bar technique.

What should I bring to the trial?

Comfortable clothes, flat-soled shoes and a water bottle. Towels and lockers are available at the front desk.

Is personal training extra?

Batch coaching is included in every plan. One-to-one sessions are available at a separate rate, ask your coach.

Can I pause my membership?

Annual members get two freeze weeks per year. Monthly and quarterly members can ask the front desk for a pause during travel or illness.
